# Crypto AlphaFactory mission

Last updated: 2026-07-17 Asia/Hong_Kong

## Mission

Build a reproducible, multi-paradigm Crypto research system that can compare data representations, targets, models, selectors, portfolio mappings, costs, and evaluators under one explicit evidence boundary.

The project succeeds through credible economic research conclusions, not through interface count, test count, Graph size, or architecture status alone.

## Research contract

The canonical causal chain is:

```text
data and field semantics
-> materialization and availability time
-> target and horizon
-> representation and model/search
-> selector
-> portfolio mapping
-> turnover and cost
-> development evidence
-> independently authorized OOS proof
```

A conclusion must be limited to the links actually verified. Loadability is not PIT qualification; model fit is not field utilization; development increment is not OOS proof; tests do not grant architecture authority.

## Current durable capabilities

- An observed-archive 18-month train-only surface joins 2023H2 and the authorized 2024 train block. It is not a survivorship-complete exchange universe.
- A finite-grammar internal search instrument, explicit portfolio mappings, strict-feasibility feedback, and mapped turnover/cost evaluator are available under bounded authority.
- Development-only compositional and explicit/latent experiments can run without opening sealed, recent, challenge, forward, or promotion roles.
- A paradigm-aware Data Adequacy Gate prevents underpowered data from being reported as a paradigm failure.
- RAW Graphify provides code navigation; CURRENT provides a compact, human-approved authority and contract overlay.

## Current research position

- Qlib's original comparison was fit-degenerate; the frozen repair removed the degeneracy, but available dates remain underpowered.
- DeepDow produced non-identical learned weights but remains data-underpowered.
- The 18-month compositional search localized development mechanisms but did not produce independent evidence sufficient for a challenger.
- The fixed residual TCN and overlapping field-family structured proxy produced implementation-specific development negatives after cost. This does not reject richer latent representations or prove all 41 fields were used.
- The repository contains a much wider static feature inventory, including aggTrades-derived volume and flow specifications, but most are not materialized on the 18-month runtime surface.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- `NEW_PERFORMANCE_SEARCH_FROZEN`
- `FORWARD_SEALED`
- no challenge, recent, May stress, or spent report-only feedback
- `NO_CANDIDATE_PROMOTION`
- `NO_CROSS_SPRINT_ADAPTIVE_MEMORY`
- no same-bar or future leakage
- no unqualified field, data release, or portfolio mapping bypass
- no economic claim broader than the reproduced object, budget, mapping, and evidence block

These boundaries may change only through an explicit accepted decision. Source presence, a passing test, or a positive development result cannot open them.

## Meaningful completion

A material project result requires at least one of:

- a reproducible external or internal component with credible development increment under a matched bridge evaluator;
- an architecture change that correctly expresses a previously impossible research paradigm;
- a fair multi-paradigm comparison demonstrating that current data, rather than generator/model/mapping limitations, is the hard blocker;
- independently authorized OOS evidence sufficient for an explicit promotion decision.
