# ADR 0002: NEXTGEN-DARK isolated observation infrastructure

Status: Accepted for infrastructure; performance integration remains frozen.

NEXTGEN-DARK materializes only approved development/pre-forward observations and gives every output explicit observable-time, maturity, missingness, lineage, cache, and feedback metadata. Typed temporal/event programs, hypothesis lanes, admission, benchmarks, challengers, and coverage are separate contracts. Lanes do not share elites, A7MEM, historical winners, archives, lineage namespaces, or seeds by default.

Liquidation state remains `UNAVAILABLE_NO_APPROVED_SOURCE`; proxies are forbidden. A later PC1 audit found Binance UM bookTicker BBO observations for a scoped 2024-01/02 subset. They qualify top-of-book liquidity with explicit missingness and +1h observability, but must not be represented as multi-level depth. Funding production qualification remains limited to Binance UM core12 through 2026-04-30.

No NEXTGEN-DARK output may reach reward, generator memory, candidate promotion, scheduler adaptation, formal search, or forward evaluation. The CANARY plan is fixed-budget, development-only, frozen-policy, not authorized, and not started.
