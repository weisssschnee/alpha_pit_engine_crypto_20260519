# Crypto Search Run Evidence Contract V1 — milestone and audit

Date: 2026-08-04 Asia/Hong_Kong

Repository HEAD audited: `815f054fe92050ce04197874246cbe838a7d7f89`

Status: `MILESTONE_RECORDED_CONTRACT_ACCEPTED_IMPLEMENTATION_AND_OBSERVATION_PENDING`

## Outcome

The next Search Engine run must produce enough joined evidence to explain why
search failed. This is an experiment evidence contract, not a new framework,
database, evaluator, compiler, scheduler, or Graph node.

The accepted chain is:

```text
data / target authority
-> actual search exposure
-> optimizer allocation
-> realized mechanism
-> matched increment
-> executable behavior
-> economic waterfall
-> authorized stability evidence
-> frozen reward-ranking audit
```

Optimizer allocation is a cross-cutting attribution dimension, not a terminal
pipeline stage. Complexity is exposure evidence, not a success criterion. A
simple mechanism with distinct matched increment and stable executable net value
outranks a syntactically complex mechanism whose extra axes do not participate.

No market search, replay, sealed read, OOS, tuning, reseed, rescue, promotion,
new grammar, or optimizer change was performed by this milestone.

## Milestone definition

### Project-level state change

The project now has one accepted, deduplicated evidence contract for a future
separately authorized real Search Engine run. Failure may no longer be attributed
only to expression complexity, reward, optimizer, data, or mapping without
evidence from the relevant stage.

### Decision unlocked

A future run can be scoped as one research experiment that reuses current
authorities and passively emits the missing evidence. Its result can select the
correct next intervention layer without another generic architecture cycle.

### Evidence required to complete the capability

1. A separately authorized run freezes target, execution price, mapping, cost,
   evidence partitions, generator policies, and exposure strata before market
   evaluation.
2. At least one real candidate row joins actual exposure, mechanism realization,
   matched sleeves, behavior provenance, economic waterfall, and the frozen
   reward identity.
3. Arm-level results retain raw-proposal and strict-evaluated denominators.
4. Missing or unauthorized stability roles are reported as `NOT_OBSERVED` or
   `NOT_AUTHORIZED`, never as zero or failure.
5. An independent checker verifies the source, contract, ledger, receipts, and
   no-feedback boundaries.

### Explicitly out of scope

- historical V2.4 stage inference or replay;
- a universal failure-attribution platform;
- a second ledger, behavior archive, evaluator, AST, compiler, or scheduler;
- changing reward, Evolution, CEM, mapping, or mutation probabilities while
  collecting attribution evidence;
- opening holdout/OOS, challenge, recent, May-stress, forward, or promotion;
- treating syntactic complexity, Graph freshness, tests, or schema completion as
  economic research success.

### Quantitative audit observations

- V2.3 did not search only simple two-field expressions: 4,038 candidates used
  two raw fields, 9,364 used three, and 2,598 used four.
- Syntactic breadth did not imply balanced realized exploration. Evolution placed
  4,383 of its 8,000 candidates in `CONDITIONAL_V2_FUNDING_FLOW_CROWDING` and
  3,283 in `MECHANISM_V2_FLOW_INTENSITY_CONVICTION`; many other families received
  single-digit or zero Evolution rows.
- V2.3 random produced 7,991 new behavior families; Evolution produced 6,667.
  Both arms produced zero strict matched-positive candidates. These numbers show
  exposure and behavior concentration, but not whether the declared axes were
  active.
- V1.4 exact Binance-target replay evaluated 600 binary and 600 hierarchical
  candidates. Gross was positive for 324 binary and 337 hierarchical candidates,
  net was positive for 15 and 17, and matched-positive remained zero. Complexity
  alone therefore did not solve matched increment or execution economics.
- V2.4 repair replay produced 140 strict evaluations and 372 final control/support
  equalities. Random reachability was 100/256 and Evolution reachability 40/256.
  The current historical artifacts cannot locate the first equality stage.

## Corrections required before treating the proposed contract as executable

1. **Do not create a framework.** Implement only thin ledger additions and one
   consumer join inside the next authorized run.
2. **Do not invent exposure labels.** `single-axis`, `stateful`, and `event` must
   be deterministically derived from existing CandidateSpec/mechanism/control
   semantics. An A or B matched sleeve is not automatically a separately proposed
   single-axis candidate.
3. **Do not freeze arbitrary percentages yet.** Freeze minimum per-stratum counts
   and report both candidate-count and process-CPU denominators. Complexity classes
   have unequal proposal and evaluation cost.
4. **Define realization through existing evidence.** An axis is not active merely
   because its input is finite. It requires non-constant eligible support and a
   non-identical existing ablation/incremental output. Do not add a new evaluator
   or uncontracted per-axis market evaluation.
5. **Use four-state evidence.** Every stage reports `PASS`, `FAIL`,
   `NOT_OBSERVED`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`; evidence partitions also allow
   `NOT_AUTHORIZED`. Missing observations are never zeros.
6. **Separate first failure from downstream diagnostics.** Once a required stage
   fails, later economic numbers may be diagnostic but cannot be interpreted as
   proof that the failed mechanism existed.
7. **Keep economics separate from scalar reward.** The waterfall is observed
   evidence. Reward is a frozen ordering rule audited against it; the same run
   cannot tune reward from its own attribution output.
8. **Preserve equal denominators.** Optimizer claims require proposal-count,
   strict-evaluated-count, behavior-unique, and process-CPU views. Survivor-only
   means cannot qualify an arm.

## Non-duplicate open questions

These are the unresolved questions that the next real run may answer. Questions
already closed by existing loadability, compiler, matched-control, Archive,
checkpoint, or V2.5 source tests are intentionally excluded.

### Target and time scale

1. `Q01_COST_FIDELITY` — Does the frozen venue/instrument cost model remain
   credible across the observed asset, turnover, and holding distributions, or
   does a generic 5 bps assumption determine the result?
2. `Q02_INFORMATION_DECAY` — Are the frozen horizon, rebalance, and holding rules
   aligned with OI/flow information decay, or do 1h/4h mappings miss a materially
   shorter or state-dependent response?

### Exposure and optimizer allocation

3. `Q03_ACTUAL_EXPOSURE` — Which semantic families and deterministic complexity
   strata actually receive proposals, exact-unique candidates, strict evaluation,
   and CPU, rather than merely existing in the catalog?
4. `Q04_OPTIMIZER_CONCENTRATION` — Is Evolution/CEM concentration supported by
   higher unconditional useful-candidate yield, or is it mode collapse toward a
   small set of syntactic/behavioral families?
5. `Q05_USEFUL_YIELD` — At equal proposal count and process CPU, which arm creates
   more control-nondegenerate, behavior-unique, strict-evaluated, and matched-
   positive candidates?

### Mechanism realization and matched value

6. `Q06_AXIS_REALIZATION` — For each declared axis, is there sufficient finite
   variation on the shared eligible support and an observable change in its
   existing ablation sleeve?
7. `Q07_CONDITION_OCCUPANCY` — Do conditional/regime mechanisms activate both
   economically relevant states often enough to constitute a real condition,
   rather than a constant or rare mask?
8. `Q08_MATCHED_INCREMENT` — Conditional on realized axes, does `AB` improve on
   both A and B, and does `ABC` improve on AB under identical target, support,
   mapping, horizon, and cost?
9. `Q09_OPERATOR_BASIS` — Which existing algebra/operator bases create distinct
   incremental behavior, and which are only syntactic variants of the same
   executable mechanism?

### Behavior transformation and field information

10. `Q10_BEHAVIOR_RECONVERGENCE` — When primary and control finish equal, is the
    earliest stable equality at SIGNAL, orientation, rank, selection, cap,
    mapping, or executable weight?
11. `Q11_LINEAGE_NOVELTY` — For existing mutation/crossover receipts, do children
    create new matched incremental behavior relative to comparable parents, or
    only new expression identities? This is secondary attribution, not causal
    proof of operator effectiveness.
12. `Q12_FIELD_INCREMENT` — Which OI/mark, flow, funding, basis, price-response,
    and large-trade clusters add behavior or matched information beyond existing
    clusters, rather than only increasing field count?

### Economics, stability, and reward

13. `Q13_ECONOMIC_KILL_STAGE` — By mechanism family, does failure begin at gross
    direction, uncertainty/LCB, turnover, cost, concentration, support, net, or
    monthly stability?
14. `Q14_MAPPING_EXECUTION_TRADEOFF` — Does an existing mapping/horizon preserve
    incremental information while reducing turnover, or is apparent improvement
    obtained by collapsing behavior back toward its control?
15. `Q15_MIGRATION` — When an authorized validation or fresh partition exists,
    did the mechanism never exist, or did a train-realized mechanism fail by
    seed, horizon, month, or regime during migration?
16. `Q16_REWARD_ALIGNMENT` — Does the frozen reward rank behavior-unique matched
    candidates consistently with equal-count validation/fresh economic evidence,
    without survivor bias or post-hoc threshold changes?

## Questions explicitly not reopened

- Can the admitted fields be loaded and compiled?
- Can existing CandidateSpec, typed compiler, matched controls, evaluator, and
  deterministic replay execute?
- Does Behavior Archive have an incremental behavior identity and champion rule?
- Can V1 checkpoint state restore exactly?
- Can the V2.5 consumer represent provenance rows and fail closed on missing rows?
- Can historical V2.4 first-equality stages be inferred without replay? No.
- Did V2.3 OOS establish broad mechanism Alpha or promotion authority? No.

## Next-run evidence rule

The next run, if separately authorized, must keep generator, mapping, evaluator,
reward, and adaptive policies frozen while the above evidence is collected. It
must not claim every question is observable: the run manifest declares which
questions are `IN_SCOPE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, or `NOT_AUTHORIZED` before data read.
The terminal report then selects the earliest supported failure layer and the
corresponding next intervention. It does not modify that layer during the same
run.
