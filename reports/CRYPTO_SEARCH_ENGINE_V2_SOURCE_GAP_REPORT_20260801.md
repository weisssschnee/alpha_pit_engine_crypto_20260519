# Crypto Search Engine V2 Source Gap Report

- The prior proposal surface was bounded by 40 fixed `Skeleton` variants; CEM and Evolution could reweight or mutate genes but could not expand economic mechanism structure.
- Economic hypotheses were implicit in skeleton identifiers, so operator choice, condition semantics, mapping class, and matched-control schema lacked one stable declarative identity.
- The existing `Expression`, `TypedExpressionRegistry`, `CandidateSpec`, compiler validation, matched evaluator, Binance target, cost model, Behavior Archive, and atomic checkpoint path are reusable; replacing them would duplicate authority.
- The missing layer is a typed mechanism catalog compiled into the existing AST, plus mechanism-level random, CEM, and Evolution policies with arm-local campaign memory.
- Final validation previously allowed random-control survival to veto other arms. V2 requires the same absolute kill-line to be applied independently to every arm.
- Durable reuse must stop at declarative mechanism specifications and aggregate evidence. Candidate populations, RNG state, CEM distributions, and individual rewards remain checkpoint-local and cannot cross campaigns.

Implementation boundary: add no second AST, compiler, evaluator, database, scheduler, Graph layer, or general AutoML platform.
