## Key Findings

1. Formula generation and feature adapter parity are mostly present.
   The A7AR-0..4 chain inherited and smoke-tested the CN-style generation, feature algebra, fresh memory keys, and selector plumbing. This is necessary but not sufficient for mature search.

2. The most important missing node is not generation. It is the shared candidate pool plus replay-aware selector lifecycle.
   CN does not let generator-local diagnostics become official candidates. It routes candidates through a shared pool, frozen selection, strict replay, global clustering, OOS/regime/marginal audit, and promotion triage.

3. A7AR-4 is only selector plumbing.
   It checks diversity, memory collision, field caps, and readiness. It does not score candidates with replay-aware selector features and does not replace CN's trained replay ranker or signal-vector selector.

4. A7AL-2U correctly identifies the immediate crypto blocker.
   The same-objective OI x price run produced pre-May clues, but A7AL-2T found all company-full candidate rows May sign-flipped and May control-dominated. Therefore the next step must be replay-aware selector dry-run, not more direct OI x price expansion.

5. Crypto has stronger negative-control discipline than the older CN chain in some places.
   Wrong-lag, sign-flip, row/time/symbol shuffle, matched-control dominance, and May stress-only policies are explicit. These should be preserved when importing CN mature-chain nodes.
